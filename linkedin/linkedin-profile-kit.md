## 11. Activity plan (30 minutes a week)

Your profile is now stronger than most graduate profiles. The remaining gap is that nobody knows it exists.

**Week 1 — one post, the hackathon.** The story writes itself: 48 hours, a real water problem, a working prototype, top six of ~100 builders. Lead with the constraint, not the congratulations. Attach two dashboard screenshots and link the case study.

**Week 2 — the operation.** "I run a 37-subscriber internet service and got tired of tracking payments in my head, so I built the Android platform that runs it." One screenshot of the admin app, one of the router queue rules, a plain-language explanation of why verified payments had to map to real bandwidth limits.

**Week 3 — the final-year project.** What it takes to design a language: lexer, parser, AST, interpreter, then 147 tests to prove the thing behaves. This one lands with engineering managers because almost nobody does it.

**Ongoing:** leave one substantive comment a week on posts from Zambian tech companies, BongoHive, Garage48 alumni and Copperbelt University staff. Comments create profile views far faster than posts do, and they compound into a network that is actually warm when you apply.

**Then:** ask three people for recommendations while the GreenCode and internship work is recent — a teammate, a mentor or organiser, and a tutoring client or someone from the network operation. Give each of them two sentences to react to; vague requests get ignored.
